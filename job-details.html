Job Description

Northwind is looking for a Senior .NET Developer to build and scale the APIs powering our core platform. You'll work with a cross-functional team to design, develop and ship robust backend services used by millions of customers.

This is a high-impact role with real ownership — you'll influence architecture, mentor junior engineers and help set the technical direction of the team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop and maintain scalable .NET services and APIs.
  • Write clean, testable code and participate in code reviews.
  • Collaborate with product and design to deliver new features.
  • Optimise application performance and reliability.
  • Mentor junior developers and contribute to technical decisions.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience building .NET / C# applications.
  • Strong understanding of ASP.NET Core and REST APIs.
  • Experience with Azure cloud services and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Solid knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and performance bonus.
  • Health, dental and vision coverage.
  • 401(k) with company match.
  • Flexible hybrid working schedule.
  • Annual learning and development budget.
